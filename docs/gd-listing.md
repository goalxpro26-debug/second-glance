# Second Glance — GD listing copy

## Description (347 chars, portal limit 500)
Second Glance is a dress-up memory game set in a small styling atelier above a night market in Kathmandu. A guest shows you their look once, then the curtain drops and you rebuild the outfit from memory. Pick tops, bottoms, shoes, hats and extras, please three fussy critics, and present the look before time runs out. 12 levels plus endless mode.

## Instructions (332 chars, portal limit 500)
Watch the guest's outfit closely during the glance, then rebuild it from the wardrobe. Tap a tab to change category, tap a garment to dress the model, tap it again to remove it. PEEK shows the look again for a short time. Read the critics' wishes, they are clues to the look. When the outfit is ready, tap PRESENT to show the guest.
